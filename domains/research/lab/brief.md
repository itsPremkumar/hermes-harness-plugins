# Research Brief — NVIDIA AVO

Every claim cites at least one URL; every cited URL must verifiably resolve.

## Claims

- NVIDIA announced on Aug 21, 2026 that its AVO agent system reached a
  100.00 RHAE score on the ARC-AGI-3 public benchmark, completing all 183
  levels across 25 environments in 6,624 environment actions.
  https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-avo-reaches-100-on-arc-agi-3-demonstrating-a-frontier-level-general-purpose-architecture-for-long-horizon-autonomous-agents/

- NVIDIA frames AVO as evidence that agent performance derives from
  system-level architecture — persistent memory plus supervision plus tool
  use — rather than from model capability alone.
  https://forums.developer.nvidia.com/t/nvidia-avo-reaches-100-on-arc-agi-3-demonstrating-a-frontier-level-general-purpose-architecture-for-long-horizon-autonomous-agents/380879

- The AVO research paper describing Agentic Variation Operators for
  autonomous evolutionary search is available on arXiv.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24517

- Independent coverage reports AVO lifted Claude Opus 5 from a reported
  ~30.2% baseline on ARC-AGI-3 to 100% inside the complete agent system,
  while cautioning this is not a controlled ablation.
  https://thenewstack.io/nvidia-avo-arcagi3-benchmark/

- Third-party analysis emphasizes the result covers the ARC-AGI-3 public
  set only, not the semi-private or fully private competition sets.
  https://www.explainx.ai/blog/nvidia-avo-arc-agi-3-100-percent-long-horizon-agents-august-2026
